Privacy Policy – Wordle

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wordle ("the App"), developed by Jeferson Siqueira ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Wordle is a fully offline word game. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App does not require account creation or login.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless experience, the App saves the following data locally on your device only using Android's standard storage:

  • Game progress and statistics (wins, streaks, guess distribution)
  • Preferences (e.g., theme)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain non-personal data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location (country/region, based on IP)
  • App usage statistics

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

4. Children's Privacy

Wordle is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Ads shown to users identified as children are limited to non-personalised ads in compliance with Google's Families Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party SDK:

  • Google AdMob – advertising (see Section 3)

No analytics SDK or crash-reporting tool is integrated at this time.

6. Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being exposed through our servers. All locally stored data is protected by your device's built-in security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
jeferson0306@gmail.com

9. Your Rights (EU GDPR and Brazil LGPD)

If you are in the European Economic Area (including Portugal) or the United Kingdom, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time via Privacy settings below the ad banner. If you are in Brazil, you have the rights described in the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Lei nº 13.709/2018). Since we do not hold personal data, requests about advertising data are handled by Google (Google Privacy Policy). For any question, contact us at the address in Section 8.
